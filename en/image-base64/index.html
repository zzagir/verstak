How to use “Image to Base64”

Get a data URI for HTML and CSS or turn a Base64 string back into an image. Image to Base64 online works for free and without registration: open the page, add your data and get the result in a few seconds.

  1. Choose a file or drag it into the window.
  2. Make your changes right on the page.
  3. Save the result with the button on the page.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. “Image to Base64” and every other Verstak tool is free, with no sign-up, watermarks or limits on the number of files.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser on your own device and nothing is sent to a server. That is why it is safe for confidential documents.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit — it depends on your device memory. Files up to 100–200 MB usually work fine on a computer; on a phone, smaller files are better.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and Linux. Nothing to install. You can also add the site to your home screen as an app.

Privacy policy

Files and text you process with Verstak never leave your device: all tools run locally in your browser and do not send your data to our servers.

What we may collect

If web analytics (Yandex Metrica, Google Analytics) is enabled on the site, anonymous technical data is collected: pages visited, browser type, approximate region. It is used only to improve the service.

Advertising

Ad networks shown on the site may use cookies according to their own policies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Local storage

The site stores your theme choice and caches its own files in the browser so it works faster and offline. You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time.

All files are processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

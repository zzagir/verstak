How to use “Add music to video”

Add music to a video or replace its original audio. Add music to video online works for free and without registration: open the page, add your data and get the result in a few seconds.

  1. Choose a file or drag it into the window.
  2. Adjust the settings if needed — sensible defaults are already selected.
  3. Click “Add audio” and download the result.

Video is processed on your device. The video engine (~32 MB) loads on first use. Processing is slower than in desktop software: a minute of HD video usually takes 1–3 minutes.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. “Add music to video” and every other Verstak tool is free, with no sign-up, watermarks or limits on the number of files.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser on your own device and nothing is sent to a server. That is why it is safe for confidential documents.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit — it depends on your device memory. Files up to 100–200 MB usually work fine on a computer; on a phone, smaller files are better.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and Linux. Nothing to install. You can also add the site to your home screen as an app.

How to use “PDF to RTF”

PDF text as RTF — opens in Word, WordPad and LibreOffice. PDF to RTF online works for free and without registration: open the page, add your data and get the result in a few seconds.

  1. Choose a file or drag it into the window.
  2. Make your changes right on the page.
  3. Click “Convert” and download the result.

Text is transferred with paragraphs and pages. Images and layout are not kept.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. “PDF to RTF” and every other Verstak tool is free, with no sign-up, watermarks or limits on the number of files.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser on your own device and nothing is sent to a server. That is why it is safe for confidential documents.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit — it depends on your device memory. Files up to 100–200 MB usually work fine on a computer; on a phone, smaller files are better.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and Linux. Nothing to install. You can also add the site to your home screen as an app.

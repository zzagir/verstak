How to use “PDF viewer”

Open PDFs in the browser with zoom and fast scrolling, even very large ones. PDF viewer online works for free and without registration: open the page, add your data and get the result in a few seconds.

  1. Type or paste your text into the field.
  2. Choose the options — the result updates instantly.
  3. Copy the result or save it as a file.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. “PDF viewer” and every other Verstak tool is free, with no sign-up, watermarks or limits on the number of files.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and Linux. Nothing to install. You can also add the site to your home screen as an app.

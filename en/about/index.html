About Verstak

Verstak is a set of 104 free online tools for PDF files, images and text. Merge and compress PDFs, convert documents and e-books, crop photos, remove backgrounds, make transliterated URLs, generate QR codes and much more.

Why it is safe

All processing happens in your browser. Files are not uploaded to a server, are not stored and are not visible to anyone — including us. You can even use the site offline after the first visit.
